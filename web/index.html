Dynamic programming

Combines optimal seam prefixes to find a minimum-cost path through the current energy map.

Greedy

Starts at the cheapest top-row pixel and chooses a cheapest legal neighbor, one row at a time.

Graph cut

Models connected seams as finite cuts and finds an optimal cut with maximum flow.
